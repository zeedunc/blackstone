# Buying a used GPU on eBay — verification checklist

Goal: get a **real, working** card, not a broken board or a scam listing.

## 1. Read the condition field (most important)

eBay has an explicit item-condition field. On a GPU listing it is one of:

- **Brand New** — sealed retail.
- **Open Box** — opened, unused/lightly used.
- **Used / Pre-Owned** — used but functional.
- **Seller Refurbished** — repaired/rebuilt, tested by seller.
- **For parts or not working** — **broken. Do not buy as a working GPU.**

If the price looks like a working card but the condition says *for parts*, walk away.
Titles like "No display output", "artifact", "not tested", or "AS-IS" mean the same.

## 2. Price sanity check

For an **RTX 3090 24GB** (as of Aug 2026, eBay US):

- Working, Buy It Now: roughly **$1,100–1,260**
- Working, auction (current bid): **$800–1,130**, final price usually climbs
- Parts/not-working: **$650–850**

A "working" 3090 under **~$850** is almost always a scam, a hijacked account listing,
or a mislabeled parts card. Too-good prices on in-demand hardware are the #1 scam signal.

## 3. Spot the multi-variant bait listing

Listings titled like:

> "Dell NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 3070 3080 3090 8 10 12 24GB GDDR6X"

show a low price that applies to the **cheapest** variant (the 3060). Select the 3090
in the dropdown and the price jumps. These are not deals — they're click-bait.

## 4. Check the seller

- Prefer **99%+ positive** with **100+ ratings** for a $1k+ purchase.
- A **0-feedback** seller on a big-ticket GPU is a red flag (new account, or burner).
- Watch for sellers whose recent feedback suddenly changes category (hijacked account).

## 5. Geographic / provenance red flags

- **Founders Edition cards shipped from China** — FE cards were never sold retail in
  China; this is a known counterfeit/repack vector.
- Card located in a country different from the shipping country = common drop-ship scam.

## 6. Ask for proof, if in doubt

A legitimate seller will usually provide on request:
- a photo of the card running (GPU-Z screenshot showing "RTX 3090" + 24 GB VRAM),
- a benchmark or `nvidia-smi` screenshot,
- the actual serial number (cross-check warranty/RMA status with the board partner).

## 7. Use buyer protection

- Pay **through eBay checkout only** — never off-platform (Zelle/Wise/gift cards).
- eBay Money Back Guarantee covers "item not as described" and "not received".
- Buy It Now listings have cleaner protection than auctions for returns.
