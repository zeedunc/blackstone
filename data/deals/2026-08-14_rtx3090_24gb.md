## How to verify before buying

- **Condition field** must say *Used/Pre-Owned*, not *For parts or not working*.
- Read the description for **"tested / working / boots / benchmarks"** vs **"untested / as-is"**.
- A **working** 3090 under ~$850 is almost certainly a scam or a mislabeled parts card.
- Buy It Now from **99%+ / 100+ ratings**; avoid 0-feedback sellers on big-ticket items.
- Founders Edition cards shipped from China are a known counterfeit/repack vector.
